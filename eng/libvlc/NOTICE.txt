LibVLC for Windows, built without GPL code by AP Solutions for AP Reelume.

This tree is VLC media player's library (libvlc.dll, libvlccore.dll, plugins/ and hrtfs/),
Copyright (C) the VideoLAN team and its contributors, built from VLC 3.0.23 with the patches and
scripts published beside it. It is modified: the build leaves out every module and third-party
library whose licence is the GPL, among them the two teletext decoders (zvbi and telx), so teletext
subtitles are not decoded. FFmpeg is also built without its H.264, HEVC and AAC decoders and its AAC
encoder: those three formats are decoded by Windows through Media Foundation (the mft plugin, which
the patches change so that it decodes H.264 from MP4 and Matroska and decodes HEVC), and HEVC needs
Microsoft's HEVC Video Extensions on the computer.
